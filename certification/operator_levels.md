# Operator Levels — Detailed Rubrics

## Apprentice Rubric (out of 100)

| Dimension | Points | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Kernel coverage | 25 | Used all 11 passes appropriately across 3 deliverables |
| Intake contract quality | 15 | Contracts capture intent, audience, voice, genre stack |
| Epistemic care | 15 | No fabricated claims; sources tracked |
| Voice fidelity | 10 | Voiceprint declared and preserved |
| Output mode use | 10 | Demonstrated clean + scorecard + at least one other mode |
| Domain breadth | 10 | At least 2 distinct arenas |
| Self-audit quality | 10 | Honest critique of own work |
| Doctrine literacy | 5 | Can articulate the v3.0 Law |

Passing: 80/100.

## Operator Rubric (out of 100)

| Dimension | Points | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio quality | 30 | 5+ deliverables, each ≥ 85 prose + ≥ 85 epistemic integrity |
| Domain breadth | 20 | 3+ distinct arenas; demonstrated mastery in each |
| Voice consistency | 15 | Voice fingerprint stable across deliverables in the same voice family |
| Genre stack literacy | 10 | Demonstrated handling of multi-pack stacks with collision resolution |
| High-stakes handling | 10 | At least one deliverable in academic / medical / legal / government / grant / financial domain |
| Teaching ability | 10 | Can explain why the system works to a peer |
| Contribution potential | 5 | Open issues / PRs / RFCs showing engagement |

Passing: 85/100.

## Architect Rubric (out of 100)

| Dimension | Points | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| RFC quality | 25 | At least one accepted RFC contributing to doctrine |
| Implementation | 25 | Merged PR adding genre pack, voiceprint, schema, or agent |
| Benchmark contribution | 15 | New benchmark cases added with measurable improvement |
| System extension thinking | 15 | Demonstrated ability to extend without breaking |
| Teaching | 10 | Can teach Apprentice and Operator candidates |
| Community presence | 5 | Active reviewer / issue triager |
| Voice fidelity (project-level) | 5 | Contributions match v3.0 voice and doctrine |

Passing: 90/100.

## Audit Procedure

For Apprentice and Operator applications:

1. Applicant submits portfolio + cover note
2. Maintainer (or designated reviewer) runs the 11-pass kernel against the submitted deliverables in audit mode
3. Compares applicant's scorecards to audit results
4. Surfaces gaps in an issue thread
5. Awards tier or sends back with notes

For Architect: by invitation following demonstrated Operator-tier contribution. No application form.

## Recertification

No recertification required. Certification reflects a moment of demonstrated mastery. Operators are expected to keep learning; the system will evolve, and operators with it.
