# v9 Note

v9 does not change fiction gates. The eight-phase compression model and its scoring addendum are craft guidance for building pressure, not a publication verdict and not a measure of authorship; a chapter that scores well on this rubric has not been shown to work for readers, which needs independent human review (see `references/diagnostics/fiction_quality_separation.md`). The phase percentages are planning aids, not a quota: a chapter whose pressure comes from different arithmetic should keep its own, and cadence in a tense passage should come from the characters' situation, not from a formula applied to sentence lengths. For memoir and reported narrative, tension is reconstructed from what happened and what the author remembers or can document; a "fatal detail" is never invented for an account presented as true.
