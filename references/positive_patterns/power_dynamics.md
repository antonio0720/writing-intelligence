# v9 Note

v9 does not change fiction gates, and this reference is a fiction craft manual: its scoring addendum rates how well a scene stages power, and a high score is neither a publication verdict nor a statement about who wrote the scene. Two reminders that follow from v9. First, the system helps an author stage the scene they intend; it does not invent a lived event for memoir or reportage. In non-fiction, a staged-power scene is reconstructed from the author's notes or recollection, and a reconstruction is labeled as one when the genre or venue calls for it. Second, an author's habitual gestures, objects and rooms are part of their voice; a catalogue entry (a drink order, a chair, a door) is a prompt for the author to choose, and the same catalogue item recurring across scenes by default is a template, not a style.
