# Reader Experience — independent observation, not self-scoring

**Status:** study schema and release gate executable; actual reader recruitment, collection and literary assessment are external human work.

A machine can count but cannot substitute its count for a person's attachment, fear, confusion, surprise, hope or grief. The reader-experience subsystem therefore records two separate things: *editorial predictions* and *observed human responses*. Prediction never becomes observation by changing its label.

## Study contract

A study binds to the exact manuscript digest, declared intended readership and genre expectation. Collect consent without putting identifiable reader information inside public release bundles. Each response should record:

- A pseudonymous reader ID and study conditions (blind reading or guided, what chapters, when reactions were recorded).
- Scene-level confusion: *What did you think was happening? What did you need to know?* Distinguish deliberate uncertainty from missing orientation.
- Attachment: *Which decision felt personal? Which person would you miss?* Avoid leading response language.
- Suspense: *Where did you want to turn the page? Where did stakes lose their consequence?*
- Pacing: stretches skimmed, reread and paused, with reasons.
- Ending: opening promises the reader believes were satisfied, unanswered questions they regard as deliberate or disappointing, residual emotional impact.
- Human evaluator's uncertainty and study limitations, including selection bias, genre mismatch and sample size.

## Experimental controls

Two readers is a *minimum editorial policy default*, not a statistical sample that can estimate popularity. For critical unresolved disagreements use another reading round, independently designed questions and scene anchors. Preserve conflicting views. Do not average contradictory reader interpretations into a fabricated single verdict. When a revised edition changes meaning or span hashes, collect version-specific follow-up, not recycled approval.

## Editorial hierarchy

`mechanical integrity` (no malformed source/edition) → `developmental audit` (structural issues evaluated) → `independent reader evidence` (experienced effect) → `human release authorization` (editor/publisher decides). The first three constrain the last; none logically imply bestseller status, award, licensing or revenue.

A reader report about a sample, mock or synthetic case is fixture evidence only. A model can assist in form design and analysis; it must not invent respondents or simulate them and then label the result an independent study.
