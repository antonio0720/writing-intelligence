# Fiction genre pack — v8 candidate addendum

**Status:** proposed. Do not overwrite `references/genre_packs/fiction.md` from the current v7 repository.

## The correction to “Character as Function”

A character needs a structural job in a scene, but that job is **not their identity**. For major characters record personal desire, unwelcome history, contradiction, private fear, existing bonds and decisions under pressure. A technically competent character can still be petty, funny, ashamed, tender, cowardly in a particular situation or wrong about someone they love. Preserve the right to a surprising but psychologically earned rule violation and impose actual consequence.

## The correction to “Show, Don't Declare”

Do not create a mechanical ban on direct emotional statements. Showing grants the reader interpretive power; telling can compress time, reveal POV self-deception, produce irony or create devastating bluntness. The criterion is whether the sentence changes perception, not whether it uses an emotion word. FI-06, FI-07 and FI-12 are editorial tests rather than unqualified regex prohibitions.

## The correction to trope-fixed thriller pacing

The five beats, eight tension phases and percentage windows are examples of ways to control response. Slow procedural dread, literary ambiguity, commercial high-action rhythm, horror, epistolary mystery and experimental nonlinearity may demand other shapes. Evaluate against the authored reader contract, maintain spatial/causal orientation, and protect intentional variance.

## The novel is a second unit of composition

The scene turn remains necessary for scene engineering, but the novel-wide arc is separately governed by `references/compiler/novel_architecture.md`. Per-scene quality cannot substitute for the book's promise/payoff closure, cost conservation, characters' emotional residue and sustained causal stakes.

## Prose preservation

A pattern alert may initiate a proposed edit but never justify automatic voice erasure. Ask what the unusual phrasing does that plain prose cannot; compare before/after on the whole paragraph and chapter; document author-approved deviations. A good technical rule protects exceptional literature, not only ordinary fluency.
