# Fiction Intelligence v8 — proposed constitution addendum

**Status:** normative proposal. Supersedes neither the v6 Constitution nor the v7 governance program until accepted through the upstream governance process. Executable mechanics in `scripts/wi-fiction.py` are labeled separately.

## The generative law

> A narrative pattern is a technique, not an obligation. Evaluate causal coherence, emotional truth, formal intention and actual reader effect against the *declared creative promise*, not compliance with an inherited scene template.

### FI-L1 — Human meaning outranks structural function

Characters participate in scene mechanics, but their identities may not be collapsed into functions or occupational roles. A character has an independent desire, emotional history, contradiction, limited knowledge and meaningful agency. The decision under cost is the unit of character proof. A scene may use unnamed background actors for simple functions without requiring invented backstories; this law targets *major characters*.

### FI-L2 — A novel is not the sum of good scenes

The novel-scale unit carries opening promises, objective changes, global escalation, losses, relationships, mystery debt, irreversible costs, emotional center and ending settlement. The scene-scale graph is necessary but not sufficient. The audit must examine transitions across chapters, acts and books.

### FI-L3 — Narrative tension is variation under consequence

Escalation changes available actions, knowledge, cost, intimacy or power. Threat intensity alone is not a direction. Relief is not failure to sustain suspense: recovery can increase attachment and make subsequent pressure credible. Fixed tension percentages are pedagogic examples only.

### FI-L4 — Mystery creates a contract of intelligible uncertainty

Conceal answers, not the question the reader is being asked to care about. A mystery ledger records its origin, clues, false hypotheses, partial answers, final answer or specifically named series carry. The balance is not a scalar "questions remaining" score because not all mysteries are equivalent.

### FI-L5 — Extraordinary power obeys declared causality

Record capability, limitations, provenance, authority, consent and consequence. A hero does not gain immunity for being a hero; an antagonist does not acquire a power to repair plotting. A contradiction is fixable by amending canon with provenance, not by pretending the original rule never existed.

### FI-L6 — Narration does not pre-solve the reader's emotional work

The writer presents perceivable evidence, consequences, behavior and sharp images. The reader makes meaning. Direct emotional declaration, narrative explanation and stylized repetition remain available when POV, irony, psychological plausibility or voice makes them necessary.

### FI-L7 — Protect exceptional prose from normalization

No automatic target for metaphor density, fragments, sentence length, scene percentage or adjective count is binding. A flagged passage can remain untouched after an author/editor provides a reason tied to its intended effect. Never rewrite only to improve detector counts or confuse authorship detection.

### FI-L8 — Fictional truth and nonfiction truth never collapse

World canon is a declared fictional constraint, not empirical physics. An invented law can be accepted as true *inside that world* without becoming an externally supported claim. Real scientific, historical or biographical claims appearing in nonfictional frames require the original Writing Intelligence external-source discipline.

### FI-L9 — Editing and evidence are different acts

A numeric alert establishes only that a measurable pattern occurred. An annotated scene relationship establishes only consistency of the annotation. A reader's stated experience is a reported experience under a given study. No one of these proves a novel is moving, beautiful, original, publishable or commercially successful.

### FI-L10 — Preserve the intentional exception, not the invisible loophole

Every accepted exception binds a rule ID, scene/work scope, decision actor, reason and evidence span. The model is not the authoritative actor; the canonical WI permission and decision mechanism must be used on integration. A world-rule contradiction cannot be neutralized by a generic taste waiver.

### FI-L11 — The ending pays for the opening

A final book should resolve a meaningful central conflict, pay off a defensible set of promises and dramatize costs in the character's world. Series continuation may carry new or secondary questions, not use the next book to replace the missing resolution of this one.

### FI-L12 — No machine publishes a work of art

Publication eligibility requires separately disclosed (a) formal correctness, (b) developmental and editorial judgment, (c) independent reader evidence, and (d) technical print/ebook proof. This supports a human release decision; it never becomes a guarantee of literary merit.

## Rule coverage and priority

The `FI-01`–`FI-60` requirements are the normative clause catalog; the original text remains attached. FI-L1 through FI-L12 establish precedence and conflict-resolution principles. Individual heuristics cannot override these constitutional requirements. Proposed release-critical obligations are flagged per rule in the catalog but remain subject to editorial judgment; optional literary techniques cannot silently become hard blockers.

## Semantic status vocabulary

| Status | What is actually known | Can release pass on this alone? |
|---|---|---|
| `UNASSESSED` | No qualifying evidence or evaluation | No, if an obligation is mandatory |
| `DATA_GAP` | Annotation insufficient; prose may still be fine | No, if a mandatory check relies on it |
| `SIGNAL` | Heuristic or declared-pattern suspicion | No, not without review |
| `DEFECT` | Explicit contradiction under provided typed world/rules or malformed release inputs | No; blocks |
| `EDITORIAL_CONCERN` | Human or recorded reviewer found a possible artistic defect | No pending resolution |
| `EDITORIAL_FAIL` | A reviewer asserted a material failure | No pending authorized remediation |
| `REVIEWED_PASS` | Human reviewer documented a satisfactory finding | Only that review obligation; never aesthetic proof |
| `WAIVED` | Scoped editorial exception exists in sidecar, without verified upstream authorization | Not valid against a structural contradiction |
| `NOT_APPLICABLE` | Human documented a reason rule does not apply | May satisfy applicability, pending authority |

## Open boundary

**Implemented in sidecar:** catalog, typed manuscript checks, subset of heuristic detectors, conservative structured signals, digest-bounded audit, policy-based gate, negative controls. **Not implemented:** universal artistic evaluator, natural-language canon extraction, native WI graph adaptation, canon-authority validation, book rendering, genuine beta-reader recruitment, external scientific fact-checking. Treat future narrative intelligence as an inspectable research program, not a shipped assertion.
