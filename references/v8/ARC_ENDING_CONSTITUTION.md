# Proposed v8 Fiction Arc/Ending Constitution

**Status:** doctrine proposed for future v8; subset executable in standalone `scripts/wi-arc.py`. NOT an amendment to the governing `references/v6/CONSTITUTION.md` and NOT accepted into main.

## Principles (A8–P8)

**A8 — Canon is the only source of fictional-world authority.** A projection can observe, classify, trace or propose. It cannot create canonical facts by writing a JSON file. No separate canonical arc database.

**B8 — Character humanity precedes function.** Role archetypes guide drama but never substitute for independent desires, flaws, dignity, loyalties, contradictions or choice. `B025` remains owned by the earlier package.

**C8 — Wants are authored observations of external pursuits; needs are attributed interpretations.** Need states carry basis and actor; model inference never silently becomes canonical character psychology.

**D8 — Arc family is not ending quadrant.** Positive-change, flat, corruption, fall, disillusionment, open and custom trajectories are valid independently of which goal or need resolves.

**E8 — The four ending quadrants are a projection, never an ontology.** Only unambiguous, author-asserted binary want/need outcomes may be classified. Partial, conflicting, unresolved, inapplicable or deliberately ambiguous results must remain unclassified.

**F8 — Every narrative state has two clocks.** The story-event interval says when the characterization is supposed to hold in the world. The knowledge timestamp says when a workspace knew this was authored. Later revisions do not rewrite earlier decisions.

**G8 — Changes carry causes and witnesses.** A turning point is a traceable revision with a scene/event anchor and meaningful consequences; a declaration of belief is not proof of action.

**H8 — History is retained.** A changed goal supersedes but never deletes the earlier goal. Historical as-of reconstruction must remain possible.

**I8 — Concession does not equal defect.** Flat arc, quiet resolution, tragedy, open ending, nonlinear plotting, non-transformative protagonist and deliberate refusal of an approved template can be artistically correct. A reasoned departure is reviewable, not automatically rejectable.

**J8 — Mechanically check what is mechanical.** IDs, time bounds, type consistency, owner references, version chains, DAGs and 2×2 projection logic are deterministic. Emotional authenticity, resonance, catharsis and literary merit require accountable human judgment.

**K8 — Separate agency from success.** A protagonist may choose, be coerced, observe, suffer, resist or fail. The machine may flag absent witness, but it must not force agency as a universal aesthetic law.

**L8 — Distinguish intent from reception.** Intended ending valence, reader-observed valence, genre expectation and editorial judgment occupy different evidence records and never merge into one numerical truth.

**M8 — Provenance survives.** The original creator of a framework, the user's interpretive notes, the owner's authored canon, the model's inference and a reader's observations must remain distinguishable.

**N8 — An edit is a proposal.** A writer may request exceptions, but only the canonical proposal / authority / simulation / commit machinery can authorize and apply a consequential semantic change. This standalone tool provides no backdoor.

**O8 — A test is not proof of quality.** Green schema validation means only that the tested input matches a shape and invariant. It is not evidence of compelling storytelling, successful publication or satisfied readers.

**P8 — Requirements have declared maturity.** `specified`, `partially_executable`, `tested_locally`, `integrated`, `shipped_upstream`, and `reader_validated` are not interchangeable. No wave is `shipped` without release-level negative controls, regression, receipt and independent readback.

## Required failure modes

- **BLOCK** when a typed projection has broken referents, invalid ordering or impossible half-open interval; CLI code `2`.
- **NOT_CLASSIFIABLE** when a quadrant lacks a determinate authored binary interpretation. Do not produce a guessed quadrant.
- **REVIEW** when a psychologically consequential declaration has insufficient witnesses or independent reader observations.
- **HOLD** for editorial/publication certification outside this tool; CLI code `3`.
- **UNKNOWN** where no canonical source readback, rights analysis or reader report exists. Absence of evidence is not a negative outcome.

## Relation to older laws

No statement here overrides v4 A–F, v5 G–L or v6 M–R. When constraints conflict, the upstream constitution prevails. This future constitution is a proposed domain tightening, not a second sovereign policy source.
