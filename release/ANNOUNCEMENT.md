# Writing Intelligence v9 — Every Edit Should Leave It Yours

**By Antonio T. Smith Jr. — Founder & CEO, Density6 LLC**

---

Every tool in this field promises to make prose sound better. Most of them do, and some of them quietly change what the writer meant on the way. A careful estimate becomes an exact number. A plan turns into something already done. A hedge drops out, a concession goes missing, and a cause nobody gave appears. The new paragraph reads better and says something the writer never said.

**v9 is built so your experience, your numbers, your positions and your voice survive every edit, and nothing goes out under your name that you did not stand behind.**

It is free, open source and MIT licensed. It runs offline on standard-library Python, needs no account and no model, and keeps every private store on the writer's own machine.

## What 9.0.0 does

- **A vault for your firsthand material.** Notes, transcripts, dated drafts and interviews, each labelled with what it is: something you observed, said, measured, sourced, inferred, imagined or planned. You can redact or withdraw any of it, and the bytes are deleted. The check holds a first-person claim with no source, a plan written up as done, an anecdote a model wrote, and an edited transcript used as a quote.
- **A meaning diff.** It lists every change a revision makes to what you meant, including a changed number or date, an estimate turned exact, a reversed opinion, a new or moved cause, a dropped hedge or concession and an altered quotation. You accept or reject each one.
- **A review ledger tied to real authority.** Decisions are recorded only under your own grant and never overwritten. A typed name alone is not authority.
- **Your voice, measured carefully.** It is built from at least three of your own samples, including how you argue, and checked on held-out text measure by measure. A match proves nothing about who wrote a passage, and the tool says so.
- **Checks before anything leaves.** It checks declared origin, consent and contributions against the venue's rules. Every consequential claim is anchored to an exact source span, and release waits for a person's decision.
- **Your own model, if you want one.** It is reached only after the venue and evidence checks pass. Whatever comes back is marked as generated and treated as a draft for your review, never a release.

## What it does not claim

The 60-case editorial benchmark has not run, so there is no quality score. An independent review by someone who did not build v9 is still open. The new tools have been tested only on synthetic material, not on a real writer's work. The meaning diff works on wording, not understanding. Nothing here can prove who wrote a passage. AI-detector scores are never a target, a gate or a claim.
