# Suspense as a Ledger of Unequal Information

**Normative scope:** C159–C167. A reader's fear is a forecast conditioned on known possible loss, proximity, uncertainty and evidentiary cues; it cannot be measured without readers. The engine can instead ask whether facts enter the appropriate perspective, whether a pledge has a causally meaningful payment, and whether a clock's expiry would change an available choice.

Model information as actor-specific: `reader_knows`, `actor_knowledge`, `used_knowledge`. An author-declared actor knowledge state is checkable for its internal consistency. Politeness and etiquette can be constraints if breaking them has material cost. Dialogue may enact threat, bargaining, concealment, consent or power transitions without moving a single body. Silence only counts as useful causal delay when something becomes newly possible or impossible.

No quota for cliffhangers, dramatic irony, short sentences or time bombs may automatically fail a scene. Independent-reader reports must be attributed as reader observations and their disagreements preserved.
