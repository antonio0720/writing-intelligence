# Opening Intelligence — Optional Techniques with Causal Receipts

**Normative scope:** C121–C131. An opening establishes a reader's predictive model, not just instant stimulation. Its contracts concern orientation, commitments and fair reinterpretation.

For each narrative opening proposal record: original genre expectation; character's current choice; where and when the audience believes they are; which facts may legitimately be hidden; source scene and projected causal descendants; what emotional, spatial and informational change happens; which genre promise can be fulfilled or consciously subverted; independent reader uncertainty where available.

A concealed clue earns a payoff when a reader can locate the original stimulus *without the author adding it afterward*. A false belief belongs to a character, a misleading camera or POV belongs to a representation policy, and authorial deceit is a more serious integrity question. The evaluator reports these distinctions but does not prescribe a twist. Quiet literary openings and deeply ironic first scenes are admissible without spectacle quotas.

The opening's narrative promises should reuse the existing ledger and graph identities for B048, B019 and the series' opening-to-ending promise ledger. This submodule never creates a second source of closure.
