# Antagonistic Systems and Bounded Foresight

**Normative scope:** C146–C158 and C171–C175. Opposing pressure can be a person, institution, nature, an internal contradiction or an interacting collection. Kind is independent of moral conduct, sympathetic interest, POV allegiance and character function. Villain archetypes are reusable descriptions; applying them does not decide a character's conscience.

An intelligent opposition requires ability, resources, incentives, knowledge, plausible alternatives and feedback. If a plan only works because an independent agent makes one exact decision, the author must establish control, an expected-distribution argument or a fallback branch. Retrospective plot success is not proof of anticipation. A mastermind who predicts infinite free choices is a defect of causality, not a badge of intelligence.

Witness statements belong to separate testimony perspectives, and harm cannot be cleared by backstory or charisma. A mentor-mask antagonist must have actual historical helpful behavior plus meaningful reveal evidence, not merely a last-minute villain label. The standalone suite tests selected structural symptoms and leaves moral cognition, testimony credibility and reader sympathy to review.
