# Craft03 Constitution — Proposed v8 fiction semantics

**Status:** designed standalone checks and proposed upstream contracts, **not** canonical amendment. The v4–v6 constitution and existing v7 receipt law remain superior until formally amended.

1. **One authored world, one owner.** Canon is `fictional_canon` state under the v5/v6 authored graph. This module is a derived, read-only projection; there is no hidden database, unreviewed authority or implicit source migration.
2. **Technique ≠ law.** An opening need not explode; a fight need not follow a four-beat sequence; dialogue may be quiet; a villain need not appear; a story may end in irreducible ambiguity. Different methods may achieve the same literary effect.
3. **Causal constraints are not taste.** An action across a blocked doorway is a testable inconsistency *if the topology is complete and trusted*. A static conversation or scene with zero recorded turn is a *review prompt*, not a logical contradiction.
4. **Decision-time knowledge is retained.** When a victory is reinterpreted as a trap, the earlier actor's perception is not silently backfilled. `known_at` is timezone-aware and old records persist.
5. **Agency is bounded.** An antagonist cannot be credited with predicting a free agent's one specific choice without established leverage, probability model, or credible contingency. A planner's retrospective success does not prove foresight.
6. **Knowledge is actor-relative.** Reader knowledge, protagonist knowledge, spoken assertions, narrator truth and institutional evidence never silently merge.
7. **Empathy does not acquit.** Harm and understandable desire are independent; suffering or sympathetic backstory does not erase responsibility.
8. **Fiction ≠ real-world source authority.** Dated claims about equipment, science, institutions, law, geography or cultural heritage require source and as-of treatment. Author invention stays in fiction realm.
9. **Cross-medium means preserving choices and consequence, not mimicking form.** A novel should not be written as screenplay merely because its action adapts well to film.
10. **No aesthetic-verification fraud.** The analyzer returns `structural_verdict`, `editorial_verdict` and `publication_verdict` independently. Publication remains HOLD unless separately proven through production and human-reader channels. The local CLI never raises that flag.
11. **Author overrides are reviewable decisions, not database erasers.** Proposed exceptions name rule, scope, rationale, version and competent approval. They may change intended craft policy but may not counterfeit a physically impossible move in existing canon.
12. **No counterfeit progress.** Authored, implemented, tested, integrated, shipped and reader-observed are different states. All upstream waves WI8-12 to WI8-23 remain PLANNED in the independent bundle.
