# C121–C180 — Acceptance Crosswalk

Acceptance is transcribed from the user-supplied requirement summary. Page references are source assertions, not independently PDF-verified. A named finding is evidence of a local partial evaluator, not upstream completion.

## C121 — Opening Strategy Repertoire

- **Contract:** Encode a selectable library of opening approaches: character entrance, antagonist entrance, relationship demonstration, catalyst, in-medias-res, flashback, flashforward, dream, puzzle, quiet power play, parallel action, iconic moment, concealed clue, and others from the notes.
- **Acceptance:** author can choose, combine, or decline techniques; compiler reports purpose and downstream obligations, never rejects an original opening for not matching a template.
- **Notes provenance:** pp.2–6.
- **Existing owner:** `references/compiler/chapter_construction.md`.
- **Local coverage:** partial_executable; upstream proposed.

## C122 — Hook-Relevance Audit

- **Contract:** Differentiate a spectacular opening that honestly establishes the work's primary conflict from unrelated spectacle optimized for momentary attention.
- **Acceptance:** map the opening event to later causal, emotional, or thematic consequences; flag disconnected prologues without demanding an explosion or immediate violence.
- **Notes provenance:** pp.2–6.
- **Existing owner:** `references/compiler/chapter_construction.md`.
