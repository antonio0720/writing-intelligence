# Real reader evaluation protocol (specified, not executed)

Use consented, appropriately supervised and privacy-protective feedback. Record who read which exact version, whether the reader knows the author or is blinded to intervention, audience language/genre familiarity, review date, questions about confusion/attachment/orientation, and a free-text verdict. Do not collapse reviewers into a single 'authenticity score'; report variation and unknowns.

## Fields

`reader_id_pseudonym`, `author_consent_ref`, `reader_consent_ref`, `work_digest`, `genre`, `language_background_opt_in`, `reading_conditions`, `confusion_spans`, `emotion_reported`, `personal_preference`, `source_factual_disputes`, `fix_suggestions`, `claimed_effect`, `confidence_label`, `retraction_or_correction`.

No real reader feedback is supplied in this package. The benchmark cases measure structural refusal, not reading enjoyment. Young-author satisfaction must be solicited without requiring identity disclosure to a public endpoint.
