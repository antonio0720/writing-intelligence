# Cultural research: provenance, scope, variant, review

**The governing question is:** who attests this, where and when, under what conditions, with which translation path and rights? Neither a user note, a wiki, a translated movie, nor a model's assertion is automatically cultural authority.

## Research record

Record `id`, `title`, `publisher`, `kind`, `retrieved_at`, `locator`, `rights`, `scope`, `status` and source version or archive digest as available. `NEEDS_SOURCE` and `SOURCED_UNREVIEWED` are ordinary legal states. `EXPERT_REVIEWED` requires a traceable qualified review record, not a model self-evaluation. Distinguish community-custodian reports, original documents, scholarly analysis, adaptations, secondary commentary, and the author's invented mechanisms.

Scope fields: prefecture, municipality, site/custodian, era, community, institution, speaker cohort, edition, collection date, narration date, event year, publication medium. `context.cultural_applicability` only checks whether declared scopes align; a match is **not proof the claim is true**. No universalization from one Kyoto site to all of Okinawa, or from a shrine to a temple.

## Variant genealogy

Folklore is often multiply attested and mutable. Preserve version A and version B with distinct collectors, locations, dates and text rights. A printed image may differ from an oral account; an anime adaptation may differ from a collection; an original author rewrite can be inspired by both without falsifying either. Never compute a 'canonical kappa' or a single taxonomy simply because a game engine prefers one.

### Living practices

Living Shinto shrines, Buddhist temples, mixed customs, Ainu and Ryukyuan traditions, religious communities and secular households have independent custodians and contemporary circumstances. Restricted practices must remain restricted. Public reference pages do not grant permission to invent private ritual details. Community consultation can change a publication judgment without retroactively altering earlier source evidence.

### Evidence preference

Use source proximity, applicability and competence, not a rigid countrywide hierarchy. For official shrine access, the present site's custodian is usually relevant. For local festivals, the current organizer and year matter. For an Ainu cultural claim, Ainu-led sources should be actively prioritized. For Japanese period history, primary period records plus interpretive scholarship can be more relevant than tourist literature. An English translation may be indispensable but it carries mediated meaning and must be labeled.

### Ethical citation and ownership

Record copyright/permission, quotation limits, access restrictions, sensitive living contributor terms and withdrawal/credit requirements. A traditional idea and a contemporary artist's expressive character design have different rights treatment. Open folklore is not a right to copy a modern illustrator's distinctive composition. The pack includes no third-party film screenshots, copyrighted page extracts or full source PDF. The six original planning seeds are proposals in a user-provided draft, not the child's confirmed intellectual property or accepted stories.
