# Sovereignty, private creativity, and age-appropriate release authority

The author decides **what the story is**. Adult/guardian authorization governs consequential **external publication and contracts**, not a license to overwrite creative text. Capability and permission are different. This tool neither knows legal guardianship nor authenticates users; a future integration must verify identity and the applicable jurisdiction and platform requirements rather than accepting free-text role declarations.

## Four kinds of control

1. **Draft control** — exactly recover the original bytes, including unconventional style and Japanese Unicode. Saving a draft does not publish it or certify it as literary work.
2. **Suggestion control** — critique-only, question-only, and research mode cannot silently rewrite the draft. A local text proposal shows before/after hashes and must be adopted by a self-declared author identity; real identity enforcement is not shipped.
3. **World control** — seed adoption, retcons, renames, arc choice, power costs and deviations from researched reality are author decisions, with clear `proposed` versus `author_adopted` states.
4. **External-effects control** — publisher account connection, public author identity, promotional bio, payments, licensing and source data transfers all require separate, revocable authorization. This package has no such effectors at all.

## Privacy minimums

No mandatory legal name, school, address, daily route, birthday or photo. The preview metadata filter removes keys `legal_name`, `school`, `home_address`, `date_of_birth`, `contact_email`, `phone`, `minor_photo`, `schedule`. **This is not a general-purpose sensitive-information scanner.** Content inside chapter prose or arbitrary metadata keys can still disclose sensitive information; a real release requires contextual privacy review.

Local file permissions (`0700` directory / `0600` objects) reduce casual disclosure on some POSIX filesystems. They do NOT constitute encryption, secure identity authentication, malware protection, per-tenant isolation in a shared app, or a promise that device backups are private. Use device-level encryption and an appropriate family-controlled environment.

## Stop conditions

Stop release on unverified authorization, unresolved sensitive identity, unattributed restricted material, consent doubts concerning interviews/photos, misrepresented factual research, or an attempt to represent an AI-generated passage as the child's original work. Do not stop private drafting merely because a fictional culture, plot, horror scene or supernatural rule diverges from a prescribed template; handle age-appropriate safety according to product-wide policy, not as a cultural authenticity judgment.

## Learning without coercion

No minimum words per day, no mandatory 12-episode season, no mandatory training/tournament/ranking, no creativity performance scalar. Optional writer-chosen goals and reviews should assess whether assistance was useful. Abandoned worlds remain available for voluntary rediscovery.
