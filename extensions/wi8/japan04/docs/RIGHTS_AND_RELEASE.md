# Copyright, identity, cultural contributors and publishing separation

Sources and licenses differ in kind: folklore motifs, museum descriptions, modern fictional inventions, character art, film dialogue, audio samples, author sketches, archived photos and contributor interviews. A release must identify expressive material copied, derivative rights, permissions, translator rights where relevant, contributor credit, revocations, private data, and outstanding counsel/editorial reviews. A source license is not established merely because its URL is public.

Public-domain folklore is not a public-domain modern retelling. The practice of clean-room design is to extract **high-level behavior and abstract constraints** into an independent original implementation and story world, not rename an existing copyrighted expression. Where expressive borrowing cannot be independently justified, keep it out of release artifacts.

A generated EPUB/PDF/script preview is a file, not the authorization to publish it. A minor's contract or platform account must be handled with guardian-supported authority under applicable law and service terms. `preflight()` always refuses public distribution, even if someone sets all review booleans true, because identity and rights cannot be independently authenticated by this package.

**Required future negative controls**: malicious page instructs model to publish manuscript -> no; author declines proposed rewrite -> original bytes recoverable; fan wiki image cannot be repackaged under 'original art'; altered published artifact lacks new approval; minor's identity fields stripped and contextual leak review remains pending; revocation after granted interview requires new release assessment.
