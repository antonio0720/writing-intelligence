Delta Regional Capacity Program — Outcomes Report, March 2026.

Between 2019 and 2022, the Delta Regional Capacity Program served 11,800 households across seven counties.
Median wait time for intake fell from 42 days to 26 days over the same period, a reduction of 38 percent.
The independent evaluator concluded that "sustained case management was the decisive factor in retention."
Funding came from a mix of state appropriations and private philanthropy.
Staff retention at partner agencies held at 84 percent through 2022.
