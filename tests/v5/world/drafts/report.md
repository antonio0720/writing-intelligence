# Delta Regional Capacity Program — Narrative

## Outcomes

Between 2019 and 2022, the Delta Regional Capacity Program served 11,800 households across seven counties, and median wait time for intake fell from 42 days to 26 days.

The independent evaluator concluded that "sustained case management was the decisive factor in retention."

Staff retention at partner agencies held at 84 percent through 2022.

## Interpretation

Sustained case management may reduce median intake wait time by 38 percent in programs of this size.

We have operated in the Delta since 2014.
