# SHIGOSEN — The Intent Commons Standard

**Arena:** landing page at /shigosen#covenant  
**Status:** approved source bundle  
**Attribution:** Antonio T. Smith Jr. and RA-OMEGA  
**Retrospective date:** July 19, 2036

## Mission

Position SHIGOSEN as the first outcome network: a finite, consent-driven network that turns declared human intent into trusted, measurable progress. This expands the existing covenant; it does not replace its promises.

## The Standout Standard

**These are world changing features.** They are not cosmetic improvements to social media. Together, they change the social object, data model, ranking authority, AI loyalty, privacy boundary, governance, economics, success metrics, and exit rights.

1. **Separate social relationships from discovery.** Relationship Ledger, Discovery Commons, Commitment Board, Outcome Archive, and Offer Commons are separate surfaces with separate authority.
2. **Guarantee relationship delivery.** A chosen audience receives a publication; no invisible throttling, engagement sampling, or paid recovery of suppressed reach.
3. **Make discovery intentional and explainable.** It is invoked for a stated purpose, returns a finite set, and names the facts, exclusions, incentives, and matching logic behind every route.
4. **End the infinite feed.** Sessions have visible boundaries. “You are complete for now” is a successful system state.
5. **Replace performance profiles with Sovereign State Capsules.** Members control current work, needs, capabilities, boundaries, availability, time horizon, visibility, audience, and expiration.
6. **Replace generic posts with Intent Packets.** Requests declare purpose, audience, expiry, outcome, confidentiality, evidence, compensation, authority, and success condition.
7. **Run a finite coordination lifecycle.** Declare → Route → Explain → Consent → Act → Commit → Verify → Preserve → Release.
8. **Turn conversation into accountable work.** The Commitment Board holds promises, decisions, assignments, dependencies, deadlines, requests, and next actions.
9. **Record outcomes, not merely posts.** The Outcome Archive preserves permitted evidence of whether a connection, commitment, collaboration, or product created value.
10. **Remove follower-count authority.** Purpose-specific relationship states and evidence replace vanity totals.
11. **Make reputation evidence, not social credit.** It is contextual, evidence-based, contestable, appealable, time-bounded, and purpose-specific; no universal human score exists.
12. **Let members own their relationship graph.** Contacts, communities, subscriptions, commitments, reputation evidence, credentials, work, and outcome receipts are portable.
13. **End advertising as ambush.** The Offer Commons is entered deliberately, and every commercial match discloses data, payment, cost, risks, terms, history, and beneficiaries.
14. **Make the economic model rise with effectiveness.** Membership, verified transactions, organizational infrastructure, authorized matching, and personal AI monetize completion rather than addiction.
15. **Give each member a Steward.** A user-owned AI protects boundaries, filters, researches, compares, requests evidence, tracks follow-through, and remains under visible authority.
16. **Rebuild communities as Purpose Rooms.** Mission, roles, rules, authority, active commitments, knowledge, completion criteria, and exit conditions replace the endless stream.
17. **Give moderation due process.** Every action names the rule, evidence, authority, duration, appeal, and restoration path.
18. **Make privacy architectural.** A Personal Data Vault grants purpose-specific, time-limited, revocable, auditable access rather than handing an application the whole person.
19. **Build a portable protocol.** Identity, relationships, intent, consent, evidence, communication, economics, governance, and AI authority can move between interfaces.
20. **Expose anti-manipulation systems.** Warnings identify artificial urgency, concealed sponsorship, coercion, synthetic consensus, abuse, and other patterns with evidence and member controls.
21. **Begin onboarding with purpose.** Ask what the member is here to do and what information may be used; earn trust through a useful act before asking for attention.
22. **Give creators channels, not performance traps.** Subscription, public knowledge, learning, community, offer, and archive channels support durable value without outrage or constant posting.
23. **Make businesses compete on transparent capability.** Members compare stated evidence, price, constraints, capacity, response time, refund policy, and outcome history with market filters they control.
24. **Replace engagement metrics.** Measure useful outcomes, needs resolved, commitments complete, trusted introductions, collaboration, disputes resolved, knowledge preserved, unwanted contact prevented, hours returned, value created, and life improvement.
25. **Start with an abnormal minimum viable product.** One meaningful goal, up to five explainable matches, a consented temporary execution room, and one verified outcome—with no feed, likes, follower counts, or ads.
26. **Make the moat constitutional, not cosmetic.** Shorter sessions, member-owned data and relationships, guaranteed delivery, transparent routing, commercial separation, and real exit rights require a different business structure.

## Public-Claim Boundary

This is the constitutional and implementation map for SHIGOSEN. The landing page must distinguish direction from shipped capability: no protocol, federation, identity, export, moderation, or commercial feature is claimed as live until its structural promise is real and inspectable.
