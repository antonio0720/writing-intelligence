## Mission

Position SHIGOSEN as an outcome network: a member-controlled coordination system that turns declared intent into trusted, consented, measurable progress. This addition expands the existing covenant; it does not replace its promises.

## Landing Copy

### The Intent Commons

SHIGOSEN is building the Intent Commons: social infrastructure organized around declared intent, verified capability, explicit consent, trusted relationships, active commitments, completed outcomes, and shared ownership.

The old system optimized the next swipe. This one asks a different question: what is this person trying to accomplish, and what is the smallest trustworthy connection that could move it forward?

**These are world changing features.**

### The Four Surfaces

- **Relationship Ledger:** chosen communications are complete, chronological by default, searchable, filterable, and never altered by paid insertion.
- **Discovery Commons:** discovery is finite, explainable, optional, and invoked by the member for a specific purpose.
- **Commitment Board:** promises, decisions, dependencies, deadlines, requests, and follow-ups become visible, consented coordination.
- **Outcome Archive:** useful results become permissioned institutional memory; expired interaction is released instead of used to pull members back.

### The Constitutional Floor

1. No invisible suppression.
2. No infinite feed.
3. No undeclared optimization.
4. No secret behavioral profile.
5. Discovery explains itself.
6. Paid placement cannot impersonate relevance.
7. Members own and can export their relationship graph.
8. Reputation is contextual, evidence-based, appealable, time-bounded, and purpose-specific.
9. AI serves the member, not the platform.
10. Completion outranks consumption.
11. Silence is not failure.
12. Exit is real.

### The Social Objects

A **Sovereign State Capsule** lets a member deliberately disclose current work, needs, capabilities, boundaries, time horizon, and visibility. An **Intent Packet** makes the purpose of a request explicit: who it is for, what outcome is wanted, what evidence matters, when it expires, and what success means.

The finite loop is: Declare → Route → Explain → Consent → Act → Commit → Verify → Preserve → Release.

### Matching Without Manipulation

Members select the matching objective and authorize the signals. Exact, Trusted, Adjacent, Opposition, Serendipity, Local, Completion, Teaching, and Emergency modes return a finite, explainable set. A person can inspect the factors, remove a factor, and see why every connection appeared.

### Steward, Rooms, Vault, and Market

A personal **Steward** protects boundaries, filters requests, prepares context, locates useful people, requests evidence, tracks follow-through, and closes expired work. It cannot publish without authority, secretly profile the member, sell behavioral data, or optimize platform engagement.

**Purpose Rooms** organize communities around a declared mission, roles, decisions, active commitments, knowledge, completion criteria, and real exit conditions. The **Personal Data Vault** grants purpose-specific, time-limited, revocable, auditable access rather than handing an application the whole person. The **Offer Commons** is a deliberate commercial market: members ask for a solution, vendors explain the match and disclose payment, costs, risks, and outcome history.

### The Commercial Starting Point

The first narrow product is simple: one meaningful goal, up to five explainable matches, one consented temporary execution room, and one verified outcome. The measure of success is not time spent. It is time to a useful outcome, needs resolved, commitments completed, trusted introductions accepted, collaborations formed, knowledge preserved, unwanted contact prevented, and hours returned to members.

### Public-Claim Boundary

This is the constitutional and product direction for SHIGOSEN. It is not a claim that every protocol, federation, identity, export, moderation, or commercial capability is already deployed. Features ship only when their structural promise is real and inspectable.
